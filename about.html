A little about me

Free spirit, forever outdoors

I'm an avid traveler and a free spirit at heart. I want to capture special moments for my clients while creating memories together that will last a lifetime.

My love for nature runs deep — my youth was spent hiking, camping, and going on road trips, and I think that's how we reconnect our souls with the nature around us. That love of the outdoors led me straight to photography. It started with wanting to take pictures of sunsets, friends around a campfire, and mountains at golden hour, and I was instantly drawn in. If nature is my first true love, photography became my second.

A fun fact: alongside photography, I hold a degree in horsemanship and have worked at ranches all over the world. It's turned out to be the perfect way to channel my love of the outdoors and find even more opportunities to photograph nature and the people who love it.
